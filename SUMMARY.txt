================================================================================
METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Risk-Neutral Distribution:
   ├── Extract from S&P 500 options prices
   ├── Use Bakshi-Kapadia-Madan (2003) method
   ├── Calculate forward price via put-call parity
   ├── Integrate OTM option prices across strikes
   └── Extract moments (mean, std, skewness, kurtosis)

Real-World Distribution:
   ├── Download historical S&P 500 data (Yahoo Finance)
   ├── Calculate monthly log returns
   ├── Compute sample moments
   └── Scale to option's time horizon

Comparison:
   ├── Side-by-side comparison table
   ├── Visual comparison (4 plots)
   ├── Economic interpretation
   └── Key insights about risk premiums

KEY RESULTS (from sample data)
================================================================================

Risk-Neutral Distribution:
   Mean             :  0.002205
   Std Dev          :  0.029411
   Skewness         : -1.845203 (negative - crash risk)
   Excess Kurtosis  :  8.235493 (fat tails - extreme events)

Real-World Distribution (scaled to T=0.0767 years):
   Mean             : -0.000963
   Std Dev          :  0.040225
   Skewness         : -0.425578
   Excess Kurtosis  :  0.126911

Key Insights:
   ✓ Risk-neutral mean > real-world mean (sample data artifact)
   ✓ Much higher negative skewness in risk-neutral (crash risk premium)
   ✓ Much higher kurtosis in risk-neutral (tail risk premium)
   ✓ Market prices in extreme events through options
